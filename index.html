Edmira Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Edmira ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile and web application that helps medical students in Nigerian universities and across Africa study, learn and prepare for their exams. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Edmira app and related services (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Edmira, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password (stored securely and never visible to us in plain text).
  • Profile details: your university and your level or year of study.
  • Learning activity: courses, topics, notes, bookmarks, quiz answers, scores and study progress.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact us for help or feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data: device type, operating system version, app version and IP address, which are processed by our servers (hosted on Render) when your device connects to the Service.

Edmira does not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS messages or call logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To deliver learning content and save your progress, scores and bookmarks.
  • To personalise your experience, for example by showing content relevant to your level of study.
  • To send important service messages such as account notices, security alerts and updates.
  • To fix problems and improve the app based on feedback you send us.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Educational Content Disclaimer

Edmira provides educational material for students. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Content in the app must not be used to make decisions about the care of real patients.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed above, to operate the Service;
  • when required by law, court order or a valid request from a public authority;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Edmira;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with access controls on secure cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal, tax or security obligations.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Under the NDPA and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data (you can edit most details in the app's profile settings, or email us);
  • request deletion of your data by emailing edmiraltd@gmail.com;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Edmira account and associated data at any time by email:

  • Send a request to edmiraltd@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Edmira account".
  • We may ask you to confirm your identity before proceeding. We will complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming your request.

Deleting your account removes your profile and learning activity from our active systems. Some anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be kept for analytics.

10. Children's Privacy

Edmira is intended for university students and users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect your data in line with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify you in the app or by email where appropriate. Continued use of Edmira after changes means you accept the updated policy.
