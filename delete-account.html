EdMira ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your EdMira account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account in EdMira, the study app for medical students developed by EdMira, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to edmiraltd@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up for EdMira.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my EdMira account.
  3. In the message, include your full name and your EdMira username (if you remember it).
  4. We will confirm we have received your request and may reply to check that the request came from you.
  5. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days of your request, and we will email you once it is done.
Email a deletion request

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account details: name, username, email address and phone number
  • Your academic details: institution, faculty, department, programme and level
  • Your study activity: quiz answers, scores, progress, focus areas and study history
  • Your app settings and preferences, such as language and appearance
  • Feedback and question reports linked to your account

Data that may be kept

  • Copies in secure backups may remain for up to 90 days before they are automatically overwritten.
  • Records we are required to keep by law, or to prevent fraud and abuse, are kept only for as long as required.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, total quizzes taken across all users) that can no longer identify you.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you want to use EdMira again later, you will need to create a new account.
